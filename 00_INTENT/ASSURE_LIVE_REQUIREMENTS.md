# What is needed to make Tessera Assure live

Three sources: the MITMARK contract lead (L), the client's Information Officer (IO), and Rich (R). Items are in the order they are needed. The first block alone makes the record real; the rest make it forwardable, defensible and reusable.

## Block 1. Makes the record real (L, one sitting)

1. **Retainer fee** per month, ex VAT, and the invoicing period. (L or R) Without it the statement shows no ratio.
2. **Contract dates**: start date, current term, notice period, renewal or review date. (L or R)
3. **What is in the retainer**: which of the twelve service lines are contracted, which are delivered but uncontracted, which are excluded. Set each line Live, Available, Recommended or Not in scope. (L)
4. **Backfill of work done**, from contract start or at least the last two quarters: date completed, service line, one plain line of what was done, outcome, statutory clock if any, estimated hours of the IO's time it replaced, MITMARK reviewer. Source: MITMARK's own timesheets, emails, the DSAR and breach registers, monthly reports issued. (L)
5. **MITMARK time spent** per item, if recorded. Not shown to the client; needed for MITMARK's margin view and for honest hour estimates. (L)
6. **Named MITMARK reviewer** for every entry, and the standing rule for who reviews what. (L)

## Block 2. Makes the figures defensible (IO, one conversation with L)

7. **The IO's loaded hourly rate**, or salary band and the share of her post that is the information role. She may prefer a band to a figure; a band is enough. (IO, via L)
8. **Her own estimate** of hours per instance for the lines she used to do herself (DSAR, breach assessment, policy refresh, vendor DPA). Her figure replaces MITMARK's assumption. (IO)
9. **What she would otherwise do**: hire, contractor, external counsel, or absorb it. Which comparator the CFO will accept. (IO)
10. **Her words**: two or three sentences on what MITMARK saves her, recorded verbatim with her consent to quote them to the CFO. This is the main effort (A4). (IO)
11. **Incidents avoided or near misses** she credits to MITMARK, with dates. (IO and L)

## Block 3. Makes it forwardable (IO, R)

12. **Client display name** as it should appear on the statement, and whether the estate's own name may appear at all. (IO)
13. **Who receives the statement**: the CFO's name and role as they should be addressed, and any second reader (COO, board secretary). (IO)
14. **Reporting cadence** the CFO expects: monthly, quarterly, at renewal. (IO or R)
15. **Format** the CFO will read: PDF by email, a page in the board pack, or the live page. (IO)
16. **Sign-off**: who at MITMARK signs the statement (Rich or L) and whether the IO countersigns. (R)

## Block 4. Makes it a product, not a page (L, R; each is a spend item)

17. **Shared store** so L enters and the IO reads from any device: authorise the £0 to £25 a month option, or supply a spreadsheet the page can read. (R)
18. **Access**: password for the page now; later, a login per reader. Who holds the password. (L)
19. **Evidence links**: where MITMARK's deliverables live (SharePoint, OneDrive, email) so each entry can link to its document. (L)
20. **Statutory registers**: the client's DSAR log, breach register and vendor DPA list, or confirmation MITMARK holds them. These feed the deadlines panel from source rather than by hand. (L, IO)
21. **Policy pack**: the current approved policies and their versions, if the defence-file export from the prior MAXIMUS build is to be revived. (L)
22. **Posture domains**: if the five-domain posture and 12-week trend from MAXIMUS are wanted, the domain list and current RAG per domain with the reviewer's note. (L)

## Block 5. Consent and control (R, IO)

23. **Client consent** to MITMARK holding a record of work about them in a hosted tool, and to the IO being named in it. Likely covered by the retainer's confidentiality terms; confirm. (R)
24. **Data on the page**: confirm no personal data of data subjects ever enters an entry; entries describe the work, not the people. Standing rule for L. (R)
25. **Who else sees it inside MITMARK**: Rich, L, Linc. Anyone else needs a reason. (R)

## What is not needed

- Her calendar, her email, or access to the client's systems. The record is MITMARK's account of MITMARK's work.
- Anything from the CFO directly before the IO has seen the record.

## Smallest next step

L fills Block 1 from MITMARK's own records into the Operator view, exports the file, and sends it back. That alone turns the beta from illustrative to real, and is a morning's work.
