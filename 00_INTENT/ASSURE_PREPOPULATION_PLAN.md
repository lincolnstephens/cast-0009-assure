# What the drive holds, and what to seed

## 1. Bottom line

The drive gives us the client's real workplan (19 activities handed over by the previous MITMARK officer in March 2026, one complete), the client's real policy set (one approved, three drafts), two live registers (RoPA, access matrix, both templates with a single row), three lawyer-drafted data-sharing documents, and MITMARK's own template library (six items). ## 4. What the seeding does not do

- It does not put client staff names, emails or the IT provider's name into the portal. Owner fields carry roles. The three lawyer-drafted documents are the client's own and are shown to the client with the client name inside them; only the titles are cleaned.
- It does not mark anything complete that the drive does not show complete.
- It does not touch the demo organisation's Record entries except to keep them flagged illustrative.
