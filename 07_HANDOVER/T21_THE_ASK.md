## (e) If the room cannot commit

They may not be able to, and the demonstration must not depend on their being able to. The fallback is not a smaller version of the ask. It is the smallest thing they can say yes to without spending anything or asking anyone.

**The smallest yes: {{SMALLEST_YES}}.**

The operator asks for it like this:

> "{{SMALLER_ASK}}"

- *Why it costs them nothing and commits them to nothing, so there is no reason to refuse.*
- *Why it still meets the main effort.*
- The operator records both answers in the two blanks. The component is designed to take a smaller answer without looking like it was refused.
- A name and no date: he takes the name and proposes the date himself. A date and no name: he takes the date and asks who will be there. He does not leave with neither.
- He does not reduce the offer, discount it, or offer to do any part of it unpaid. The ask stands; only the next step gets smaller.

**What "no" is allowed to mean here.** Not yet is a description of the first path. The next action and the next date are G5's sequels at T23, and none of them requires a promise on screen we cannot keep.
