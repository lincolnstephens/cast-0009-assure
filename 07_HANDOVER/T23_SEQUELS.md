# SEQUELS: THEY SAY YES, THEY SAY NOT YET, THEY SAY NO

*Frontmatter for the instantiated document also carries: cell G5 Plans; status ISSUED and who it hands to; date; authority, G5 delegated in full, with the statement that no promise here requires spend or client contact to keep; constraint, one line each, on figures and on naming any other client.*

Owned by G5. Each sequel has a next action and a next date, and none requires a promise we cannot keep. The three are written before H-hour so that none of them is invented in the hour after it.

---

## 1. They say yes

**In the room.** *What the operator does with the answer: records it in the blanks, reads it back to confirm, thanks the room. What he does not discuss: scope, price or team beyond what T21 THE_ASK already says.*

**Within 24 hours.** *Who sends what to whom. Written confirmation of the offer, the artefacts and the agreed next date, with no figure. Who is copied and in what role. Sending is a hard ceiling and waits for Sunray.*

**Within one week.** *The first working contact: who is on it, what it confirms, and what is explicitly not discussed until the boundary is agreed.*

**Next date proposed.** *A named event with a date or a window, held in the room if possible.*

**Owner at MITMARK.** *Who holds the relationship and who leads delivery. Two names or roles, not one.*

**Must not be promised.** *One line each: any start date before scope is confirmed; any figure; any access to or ingestion of the subject's systems or data before the governance boundary is agreed; any claim to be the sole builder or operator.*

---

## 2. They say not yet

**In the room.** The operator takes the smaller ask verbatim from T21 THE_ASK (e). He does not discount, shrink or offer any part of the offer unpaid.

**Within 24 hours.** *A short note confirming what was discussed and anything already agreed. No new material, no attachment beyond what was shown in the room.*

**Within one week.** *One follow-up, once, if the smaller thing was not agreed in the room. Named contact only.*

**Next date proposed.** *The smaller thing itself, inside a stated window.*

**Owner at MITMARK.** *Relationship holder; who drafts and reviews all written follow-up.*

**Must not be promised.** *That the offer stays open indefinitely. Any commitment on price or timing ahead of the next meeting. Contact with anyone not named by the room.*

---

## 3. They say no

**In the room.** *Thanks, no second argument, one honest question about a better time or a different problem. He leaves without pressing.*

**Within 24 hours.** *The outcome and the stated reason are logged for MITMARK's own record. No message goes out.*

**Within one week.** No contact, unless they initiate it. The relationship is protected, not chased.

**Next date proposed.** None fixed. *An internal reminder only, at a stated interval, tied to any milestone the CIR register identifies on the subject's own programme.*

**Owner at MITMARK.** *Relationship holder decides if and when to re-approach; who holds the internal reminder.*

**Must not be promised.** Nothing. No further material, no discount, no repeat approach without a new reason to believe the answer would change.
