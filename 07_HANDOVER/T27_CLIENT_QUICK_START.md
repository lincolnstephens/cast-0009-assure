## What the portal is

The portal is MITMARK's record of the data protection and information security work we do for you under the contract. It shows what has been done, what is in hand, what is waiting on you, and what it has saved you. You do not have to keep it up to date: MITMARK does. Everything you see has been reviewed by MITMARK first.

## Signing in for the first time

1. Your MITMARK contract lead sends you the sign-in address and, in a separate message, a one-time password.
2. Open the address, enter your email address and the one-time password.
3. The portal asks you to choose your own password straight away. Choose one you do not use anywhere else. The one-time password then stops working.
4. If you forget your password, ask your MITMARK contract lead to reset it. You will be sent a new one-time password the same way.

## The six menu items

- **Compliance Tracker.** Where things stand across the eight modules, what is coming up, and what is waiting on you.
- **Compliance Documents.** Your policies, procedures and trackers, to read and download. You can also send MITMARK a document from here.
- **Job management.** Every task by where it stands. A green tick is done, a grey circle not started, an amber circle in progress, and red means overdue or blocked. The word is always beside the mark.
- **Requests and clocks.** Raise a data request or report a concern, and follow its statutory deadline.
- **Statement for the CFO.** A printable statement of the work done and what it saved, for your finance director.
- **Quick questions.** Ask a question about your policies and procedures. Answers quote the documents they come from.

## What you can do, and what MITMARK does with it

| You | MITMARK |
|---|---|
| **Confirm a task** assigned to you is done (Job management, or "Waiting on you" on the tracker). | Checks it and closes it, or reopens it. Either way there may be a short note, shown as "MITMARK:" on the task. |
| **Submit a request**: a data request (someone asking for their data) or a concern (a possible incident). | Starts the statutory clock at once, picks the request up and works it to the deadline. Any note shows as "MITMARK:" beside the request. |
| **Upload a document**, choosing Policy, Procedure or Tracker. | Reviews it. Nothing is published until MITMARK accepts it and files it under the right module. If it comes back, you see the reason. |
| **Ask a question** in Quick questions. | The answer comes from your published documents. If it needs a person, use "Send to MITMARK" and MITMARK replies in the same place. |

You cannot change the workplan, and you do not need to. Tell MITMARK and MITMARK will change it.

## Who to contact

Your MITMARK contract lead, for anything about the portal or the service. For a possible incident, raise it under Requests and clocks first, then call your MITMARK contract lead.
