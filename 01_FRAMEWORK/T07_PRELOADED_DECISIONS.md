*These are the questions Seagull would otherwise bring to Sunray during the session. The standing questions D1 to D13 below are asked of every operation and must be answered before a session opens. Seagull writes a recommendation in every row; Sunray marks each AUTHENTICATED, ALTERED (with the change) or STRUCK. Extend with D14 onward per iteration, each new line stating which earlier decision it amends and each carrying the same three columns.*

**Precedence against the Phase 0 selector.** CAST-001 introduced `00_INTENT/PHASE0_SELECTOR.md`, which did not exist when these thirteen standing questions were written. Four of them now overlap it: D4 exposure with the selector's output destination, D7 spend with its spend ceiling, D11 permitted sources with its capability categories and data domains, and D13 material with what Sunray places. **Where a row here and the selector disagree, the selector wins**, because it is the enforceable authority record COI-001 section 7 requires and this document is working authority only. The rows are kept rather than deleted because each asks something the selector does not: D4 asks about the deployed address and metadata, D7 about whether a paid service may be used at all, D11 about the grading standard for anything not named, and D13 about the specific files. Answer them as the operational detail beneath the envelope, never as a widening of it.

**The two hard ceilings are not preloadable.** No row in this document authorises spend above the cap or contact with a third party, whatever it says and however Sunray marks it. Both wait for Sunray by IRTB, however long it takes.
